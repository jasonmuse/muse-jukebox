Tips

  • Press Play, then the repeat button to keep songs rolling on their own, even with your screen off. Shuffle shows up once it's on.
  • Use the skip buttons next to the record to jump to the previous or next song.
  • Turn your phone or tablet sideways for the full jukebox deck.
  • On screens with a side strip (like a folded Duo), the music controls live in the strip. Drag them up or down to move them.
  • Tap the record to pause, or use the 10s buttons to skip around.
  • Grab the record and drag it back and forth to scratch like a DJ.
  • Tap any tag or year on a song to filter the list.
  • The × on the playing song stops it and clears it away.
